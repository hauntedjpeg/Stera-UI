## Design

<DesignGuidelines>

### When to use

- A self-contained on/off control that takes effect immediately, such as bold formatting, muting audio, or showing a panel.
- A compact, button-like control that sits with other actions in a toolbar or editing surface.

### When not to use

- A persistent setting in a form or settings list. Use a [Switch](/components/switch).
- Several related options, such as text alignment or bold, italic and underline. Use a [Toggle Group](/components/toggle-group).
- An action that doesn't leave anything on or off, such as Save. Use a [Button](/components/button).

### Guidelines

- Give an icon-only toggle an accessible label and a [Tooltip](/components/tooltip). An icon alone rarely says both what the toggle does and whether it's on.

See also: [Selection](/guidelines/choosing-components#selection) and [Theming](/guidelines/theming).

</DesignGuidelines>
