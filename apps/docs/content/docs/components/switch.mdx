## Design

<DesignGuidelines>

### When to use

- Turning a standalone feature or setting on or off where the change applies right away, such as notifications or dark mode.
- When on and off describe the choice more clearly than checked and unchecked.

### When not to use

- A choice that only takes effect after a form is submitted or further steps. Use a [Checkbox](/components/checkbox).
- An on/off control in a toolbar or editing surface, such as bold. Use a [Toggle](/components/toggle).

### Guidelines

- Always pair a switch with a visible label that names the setting, not the switch's state: "Airplane mode," not "On."

See also: [Selection](/design/selection) and [Theming](/design/theming).

</DesignGuidelines>
